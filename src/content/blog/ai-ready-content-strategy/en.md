AI discovery starts with clear structure and reliable metadata.

## What matters most

- Publish focused, topic-driven pages.
- Use structured data on key templates.
- Keep technical files (`robots.txt`, `sitemap`, `llms.txt`) updated.

This creates better retrieval signals for both crawlers and answer engines.
